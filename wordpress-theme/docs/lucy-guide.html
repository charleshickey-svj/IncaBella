Updating your IncaBella website

Everything below is done from your WordPress dashboard. Log in at your-website-address/wp-admin with your usual username and password. Nothing you change here can break the design, so feel free to experiment.

1Change a price

  1. In the menu on the left, click Hire items. You'll see every item with its photo and price.
  2. Click the name of the item you want to change.
  3. On the right, find the Price & details box. Type the new number in Price (£), for example 25 or 6.95. There's no need to type the £ sign.
  4. Change Shown after the price if you need to, for example each, per table or per 10.
  5. Click the blue Save button at the top right. The website updates straight away.
Tick Show “From” before the price for packages where the price can vary. Prices mentioned inside the description, such as “Add greenery for £75”, are changed by editing that text on the left.

2Swap a hire item's photo

  1. Open the item from Hire items, as above.
  2. On the right, scroll to the Photos box. The photo with the green border is the main photo shown on the Hire page.
  3. Click Add photos. Either drag a new photo from your computer onto the window, or pick one from your Media Library. Then click Add these photos.
  4. Drag your new photo to the first place to make it the main one. Click the × on any photo you no longer want.
  5. Click Save.
When you upload a photo, fill in Alternative text on the right with a short description (e.g. “White roses and daisies in a glass vase”). It helps Google and people using screen readers.

3Swap a page photo or the home page slideshow

  1. Go to Appearance → Customise → IncaBella.
  2. Choose the page: Home page photos, Flowers page photos, Hire page photo or About page photos. Each photo on the page is listed with its current picture.
  3. Click Change image under the one you want to replace, then pick or upload a photo. You'll see it on the preview straight away.
  4. Click the blue Publish button at the top. To go back to the original photo, click Remove, then Publish.
For the big banners and the slideshow, use landscape photos at least 1600 pixels wide. Photos straight from your phone are perfect; Instagram copies can look soft at that size.

Other handy things

Add a new hire item

Hire items → Add hire item. Type the name and description, then fill in Price & details, choose its category, add photos, and click Publish.

Hide an item for now

Open it, click Published in the right-hand panel and switch it to Draft. It disappears from the website but isn't deleted.

Change the order

In Hire items, hover over an item and click Quick Edit. Items with a lower Order number appear first within their category.

Home page packages

Tick Feature on the home page in an item's Price & details box. Up to three featured items show in “Decoration packages”.

Enquiries

Every message from the Contact page is emailed to you, and a copy is kept under Enquiries, so nothing is ever lost. Just reply to the email to answer the couple.

Contact details

Appearance → Customise → IncaBella → Contact details: your email (where enquiries go), phone, address, Instagram and Facebook.

Reviews

Customise → IncaBella → Reviews. Add up to three real reviews from couples. The “Kind words” section appears on the home page once one is filled in.

Gallery photos

Pages → Gallery. Use the Gallery photos box: Add photos, drag to reorder, × to remove, then Save.

If something isn't working

  • Enquiry emails aren't arriving: check Enquiries. Every message is there, and the “Emailed to you” column says whether the email went. If it says it failed, install the free WP Mail SMTP plugin and connect it to your email account.
  • A page shows “Page not found”: go to Settings → Permalinks and click Save Changes without changing anything.
  • A change doesn't show: refresh the page. If your website uses a caching plugin, clear its cache.
