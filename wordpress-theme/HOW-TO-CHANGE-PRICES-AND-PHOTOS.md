# How to change a price or a photo on the IncaBella website

You do everything from the WordPress dashboard. You don't need to touch any code.

## Getting to the dashboard

1. Go to your website address with **/wp-admin** on the end, for example `incabella.co.uk/wp-admin`.
2. Log in with your username and password.
3. In the dark menu down the left-hand side, click **IncaBella**.

## Changing a price

1. Click **IncaBella**, then **Hire prices & photos**.
2. Type part of the item's name in the **Find an item** box, for example *lantern*.
3. Click the item to open it.
4. In **Price (£)**, type the new price as a plain number, for example `25` or `10.50`. Leave out the £ sign and any commas.
5. Scroll to the bottom and click the blue **Save changes** button.

You'll see **Saved** at the top of the page. The new price shows straight away on the Hire page, the item's own page and in visitors' lists.

Wording such as "per table" or "From" stays as it is. You only change the number.

If the item's description also mentions prices (for example "10 for £95"), change those in the **Description** box in the same place.

## Swapping a hire item's photo

1. Click **IncaBella**, then **Hire prices & photos**, and open the item.
2. Under **Photos**:
   - **Replace** swaps that photo for a new one.
   - **Add a photo** adds another one at the end.
   - **Remove** takes a photo away. Every item keeps at least one.
   - **Put the original photos back** undoes all your photo changes for that item.
3. When you click Replace or Add a photo, a window opens:
   - To use a new photo from your computer or phone, click **Upload files**, then **Select Files**.
   - To use a photo you've uploaded before, click **Media Library**.
   - Click the photo, then click **Use this photo**.
4. Click **Save changes** at the bottom of the page.

The first photo is the main one. It's the one shown on the Hire page.

## Swapping a large photo at the top of a page

1. Click **IncaBella**, then **Hero photos**.
2. Find the photo you want to change, for example *Home page slideshow: photo 1*.
3. Click **Replace**, pick or upload a photo as above, then click **Use this photo**.
4. Click **Save changes**.

Wide (landscape) photos look best here. **Put the original photo back** returns the original.

## Good to know

- **Photos are trimmed to fit.** The site crops each photo to the same shape as before, so keep the important part near the middle.
- **Nothing else moves.** Changing a price or photo doesn't change the layout or the animations.
- **Upload says the file is too big?** Your web host sets the size limit. Ask them to raise it, or send the photo to yourself at a smaller size first.
- **Changed something by mistake?** Type the old price back in, or use **Put the original photo(s) back**, then click **Save changes**.
